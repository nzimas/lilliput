Firmware for M-VAVE FM-1

No-input mixer, with drums

Lilliput turns FM-1 into a no-input mixer: 6 channels with nothing plugged into them, feeding back into themselves and into each other, through tubes, op-amps, filters, limiters and a rack of effects. The circuit is the instrument. You play it by finding where a loop starts to sing.

Beside it sits a 4-voice drum synthesizer and rhythm engine on a single shared tempo, so feedback can be played as drones, as texture, or chopped and pushed into rhythmic noise.

What it does

6 channels
Each with input selector, noise, gain, choice of tubes and op-amps, wavefolder, high and low filters, resonant middle filter, brickwall limiter whose look-ahead sets the loop's pitch, 2 sends, level and pan. Every channel can feed itself, mix bus, sends or any other channel.
Rack
7 effect slots: on channel, on send loop, or on output. Delay, flanger, chorus, bitcrusher, resonator, ring and cross modulation, octaver, reed, sag, cuts and more. Output adds equaliser and SPACE, reverb with 6 rooms.
Recipes
HOME draws a whole new patch: new connections, new settings and new motion, from any of 25 recipes (DRONE, SIREN, GEIGER, ENGINE, CAVERN, FREE and more). Every patch sounds at once. SHIFT + HOME stays in the recipe on screen.
Motion
2 LFOs and 8-slot modulation matrix on every channel, so patch moves by itself.
Rhythm
Rhythm engine that gates, chokes or strikes loops in 16 styles, from 4-on-the-floor to Turing machines.
Drums
4 synthesized voices, kick, snare, hats and metallic percussion, each with its own drive, on rhythm's tempo and beat. 28 sounds and 12 rhythms written for rhythmic noise and industrial techno.
Performance
Gesture loop on every channel, 16 snapshots with morphing, and 64 sessions in FM-1's flash, which you can back up to a computer.

Getting it

Lilliput is free software (GPL-3.0). You need an M-VAVE FM-1, a USB cable that carries data, and a desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera: installer runs in the browser and writes the firmware over USB MIDI. It runs on both of the FM-1's processor cores. Manual covers every control, and changelog every build.

Lilliput is unofficial: not made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. Installing it may void your device's warranty. Its design follows Igor Vasiliev's NoInputMixer app.
