Screens

Lilliput on the FM-1's 240-pixel screen, drawn here by the same code that draws it on the device.

Starting

The name, drawn on one grid, and the build. Lilliput starts with a patch already sounding, drawn from any recipe, at a tempo drawn too.

The main view

The chosen channel's number in its colour, its input, the patch's name, its meter with the fader's place, and its pan. A channel with nothing of it reaching the output turns grey.

Turning a knob

On the main view the four knobs reach GAIN, FREQ, LIMIT and SEND 1 (with SHIFT: NOISE, MID, PROTECT, SEND 2), each shown as a dial while it turns. LIMIT is the limiter's look-ahead: the loop's pitch.

A channel's pages

OCT− and OCT+ go through AMP, NOISE, EQ, SEND and FX: four rows a page, one per knob, with SHIFT for the switches.

EQ

HIGH, LOW and the middle filter: where a loop rings. A peak or a narrow band is how you tune it.

FX

The channel's effect slot: on the channel as an insert, or on a send loop into a return.

The overview

ARP: the six channels at once, their inputs and levels as they sound.

Motion

SEQ goes on from the output's pages to the chosen channel's two LFOs and its modulation matrix.

The matrix

Eight slots a channel: where each comes from, where it goes and how far.

The compressor

SEQ's first page: it holds the mixer's sound together and brings it forward against the drums.

The output

The main level, the two returns and SPACE, the reverb, then the output's EQ and its effect slot.

The drums

The third black key from the right opens them. White keys 1 to 4 choose KICK, SNARE, HATS or PERC; SHIFT + one draws a new sound for it.

A voice's second page

BEND, COLOR, CLICK and PAN; with SHIFT, METAL and the noise's filter.

The drum rhythm

Its name and its swing. PRESETS goes through the twelve rhythms.

A muted voice

A voice's white key held mutes it; its key blinks while it is muted.

Tempo

The rhythm's key held: TEMPO, SWING, CHAOS and the gates' LENGTH, shared by the rhythm and the drums.

Rhythm mix

How much of the gates is heard against the loops running on.

Snapshots

Sixteen states of the whole mixer on the white keys, recalled at once or morphed into over up to thirty seconds.

Gesture loops

REC records what your hands do to a channel and plays it back, round and round; each channel has its own loop.

Recipes

PRESETS turns through the recipes, the name changing in place; SHIFT + HOME draws from the one named.

A new patch

HOME: a whole new patch from any recipe, sounding at once.

Sessions

SAVE held keeps the session: the patch, its snapshots, its gesture loops, its drums and its compressor. Sixty-four of them in the FM-1's flash.
